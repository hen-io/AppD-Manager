    Performance

    Uses the graphics card for drawing and video wherever possible. Switch it off if the app shows glitches or an empty window.

    While you are not using the app

    A paused app does nothing at all: no new messages, no notifications, no playback, until you click it again. It keeps showing what was on screen. Until the pause starts, the CPU limit applies.
