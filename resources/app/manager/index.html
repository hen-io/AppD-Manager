      SponsorBlock

      What happens when a video reaches a part of that kind, and its colour on the seek bar. "Ask" shows a box with a Skip button for as long as the part plays. It counts how often a segment is skipped. Looking a video up never tells it which video you watch; this does.
      Written like Enter, Backspace, K or Ctrl+Shift+K. The skip key acts on the box that is showing.
      Performance Uses the graphics card for drawing and video wherever possible. Switch it off if the app shows glitches or an empty window.
      Slow down when not in use Not focused: about 10% of full speed, or less if the limit below says so. Not while the app is playing sound.
      Pause when not in use A paused app does nothing at all: no new messages, no notifications, no playback, until you click it again. It keeps showing what was on screen. Until the pause starts, the slow-down above applies. For apps that show live data. After the app was slowed down or paused, the updates it missed are dropped instead of played back in a burst; it reconnects and shows the current state. Anything still loading at that moment starts over.
